# Trademark policy

The source code of this repository is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. That
licence covers the **code**. It does **not** grant any right to use the
**Pulsars name, logo or visual identity** (the "Pulsars marks").

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your software is "based on Pulsars API", "compatible with the
  Pulsars API" or "a fork of Pulsars API", in plain text.
- Refer to Pulsars in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews.
- Keep the Pulsars name in copyright notices, licence files and commit
  history.

## What needs written permission

- Running a public service under the Pulsars name or logo, or a confusingly
  similar name (such as "Pulsar Science" for a learning platform).
- Using the Pulsars logo in your product, website or marketing.
- Suggesting that the Pulsars project endorses or operates your service.

## If you fork

Rename your fork and remove the Pulsars logo before deploying it publicly.
This keeps users from confusing your service with `pulsars.science`, whose
content, accounts and data you do not operate.

## Contact

Questions and permission requests: open an issue titled "Trademark question"
or contact the maintainers through https://pulsars.science.
